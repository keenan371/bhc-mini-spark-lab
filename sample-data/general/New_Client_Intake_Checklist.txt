NEW CLIENT INTAKE CHECKLIST (FICTIONAL)
Blewitt, Again & Associates LLP

Before the consultation (front desk):
1. Caller's full legal name, date of birth, phone, email.
2. Names of anyone else involved (passengers, other drivers, victims) for the CONFLICT CHECK. An attorney must clear the conflict check before any case details are discussed.
3. Arrest date and county. If the arrest was in the last 10 days, flag it URGENT (license hearing deadline).
4. Next court date, if the client has one.

Ask the client to bring:
- The DUI citation(s) and the Notice of Suspension
- Bond / jail release paperwork
- Any breath or blood test printout they were given
- Insurance information if there was a crash
- Names of witnesses (friends, bartender, rideshare receipts)
- Proof of employment if they need to drive for work

After the consultation (paralegal):
- Open the matter, complete the DUI Client Intake, calendar every deadline, send the welcome letter.

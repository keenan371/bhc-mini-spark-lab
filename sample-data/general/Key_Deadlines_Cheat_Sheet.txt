KEY DEADLINES CHEAT SHEET (FICTIONAL FIRM, FLORIDA-STYLE RULES)
Check every deadline against current law before relying on it.

LICENSE (administrative case, separate from the criminal case)
- 10 calendar days from the arrest/notice of suspension: request a formal review hearing with the state motor vehicle department. Miss it and the suspension stands.
- Once requested, the department must schedule the hearing within 30 days.
- A 42-day temporary/hardship permit may be available after the request is filed.

CRIMINAL CASE
- Arraignment: usually a few weeks after arrest. A written plea of not guilty can often be filed instead of attending.
- Discovery: request everything right after arraignment (reports, videos, breath-test records).
- Motions (for example, to suppress): file before the pretrial hearing.

FIRM RULES
- Every deadline goes on the shared calendar the same day it is learned.
- Two reminders: 7 days before and 2 days before.

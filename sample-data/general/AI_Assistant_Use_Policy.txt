AI ASSISTANT USE POLICY (FICTIONAL)
Blewitt, Again & Associates LLP

1. The firm AI assistant runs on the firm's own private box in our office. Client information never goes to public AI websites.
2. Each staff member uses only the assistant assigned to their group. It can only read documents that group is allowed to see.
3. The assistant drafts and summarizes. A licensed attorney reviews anything legal before it goes to a client, a court, or opposing counsel.
4. Never trust a case citation or a deadline from the assistant without checking the source.
5. Chat history is kept and may be reviewed by the managing attorney.
6. Report anything strange (wrong answers, access to something you should not see) to Barry the same day.

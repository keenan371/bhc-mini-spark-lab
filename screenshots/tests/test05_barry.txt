For State v. Swervington, the possible issues are related to the field sobriety tests conducted on a sloped shoulder in the rain and the observation log showing a 14-minute gap between the field sobriety test and the breath test. The calibration records are still outstanding, and both breath results are only slightly above the legal limit (0.091 / 0.089).

The plea options are:

Motion to suppress the breath results (observation period, calibration).
Negotiate a reduction to reckless driving.
Go to trial on the field sobriety test conditions.
Plea as charged.

The recommendation is to wait for the calibration records, file the motion to suppress if supported, and then use it to negotiate option 2.

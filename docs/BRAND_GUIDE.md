# BHC Private AI Offer: Brand Guide

Applies to EVERYTHING for this offer: GitHub repo, long form, every short, carousels, thumbnails, sales slides, the DC send-off, and the AI Governance & Compliance Check.

## Look
- Pure black background, white pixel line art, retro terminal feel.
- Accent colors only: BHC blue #2f8cff (terminal text, UI glow, underlines), red = ACCESS DENIED, green = ACCESS GRANTED.
- Terminal touches: `blewitt@lawfirm:~$` or `C:\BHC>` prompt line, scan lines, pixel borders.
- Big, readable characters. Phone first.

## The firm
- Name: **Blewitt, Again & Associates** (exact spelling, with the comma)
- Tagline: "Bad decisions. Excellent representation."

## The story (core message)
The client's mom calls the front desk asking about her son's breath test. Rhonda asks the firm's AI. ACCESS DENIED: "I don't have access to that information." She takes a message. Barry asks the same question. ACCESS GRANTED. **Same system, same question, different person, different answer.** The AI runs on the firm's own machine.

## Hard rules
- No children. All adults.
- No real brand logos or product photos (no NVIDIA, OpenAI, Microsoft).
- One box, not a cluster. Lab = a laptop playing the box. Don't overclaim.
- No courtroom wins or "favorable results". The story is about who sees which files.
- Clean language in business content. No em dashes.
- Every fact must match the lab (8/8 tests, 5m40s to ~11s, internet-off test only if filmed).

## Who makes what
- ChatGPT: people and scenes (characters, poses).
- Claude: terminal screens (DENIED/GRANTED), diagrams, graphs, banners, captions, animation (Remotion), repo.
